01 / ABOUT ME

Curious about the world. Attentive to what connects us.

I first came to Sweden from China for my studies. I also wanted to see the “world” — to step beyond what was familiar, meet new ways of thinking, and discover what life might look like somewhere else.

When people talk about living between cultures, the conversation often begins with differences. I understand why. But over time, I have found myself noticing more and more similarities: the things we care about, the questions we ask, and the ways we try to understand one another. That curiosity continues to shape how I see the world.

Professionally, I am a postdoctoral research fellow at the University of Oslo with a PhD in Education from the University of Gothenburg. I explore how students’ self-beliefs, teaching practices, and learning environments relate to educational opportunities. Before and alongside research, I have taught in Swedish schools, university classrooms, and multilingual settings.

This website brings those sides of my life together: the researcher, the teacher, and the person still learning from living across cultures.

02 / MY JOURNEY

Leaving to explore. Learning to connect.

My path from China to Sweden and Norway began with studies and curiosity. The milestones tell one story; the perspectives gained along the way tell another.

✳

Why I left China

I wanted to study, but I also wanted to see the “world”. Moving to Sweden opened a new chapter in both my education and my everyday life. As I encountered different languages and educational contexts, I found my attention shifting: rather than only comparing what makes cultures different, I became increasingly interested in what people share.

2010–2014

CHINA

Learning to become a teacher

I studied English and teacher education at Jiangxi Science and Technology Normal University. Education became the thread connecting many of the places I would later call part of my journey.

2014–2016

GOTHENBURG, SWEDEN

Studying, and seeing more of the world

I continued my studies at the University of Gothenburg, completing an MSc in Education in International and Comparative Education. Coming to Sweden was both an academic choice and an opportunity to experience life beyond China.

2015–2019

SWEDEN

Learning through teaching

I worked in Swedish schools, teaching mathematics, English, and Mandarin, and supporting multilingual pupils. These classrooms gave me opportunities to learn from people with different languages and experiences.

2019–2024

GOTHENBURG, SWEDEN

Turning questions into research

In my PhD in Education, I studied mathematics self-concept and self-efficacy using PISA data. I also taught future teachers and continued exploring how learning is shaped by its context.

2025–PRESENT

OSLO, NORWAY

A new place, continuing questions

As a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Oslo, I study academic resilience in mathematics and science. My work remains rooted in a question that crosses borders: what helps students flourish, even when circumstances are challenging?

✳

What I keep discovering

Culture is often described through contrasts. My experience has gradually drawn my attention to similarities instead. I hope this space will grow into a collection of reflections on learning, language, and the ways people connect across places.
